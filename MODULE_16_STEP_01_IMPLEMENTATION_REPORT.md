### 6. FINANCIAL PRECISION POLICY & MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS
Implemented in `ProfitabilityMathUtils.kt`:
- **Precision**: Strict `BigDecimal` arithmetic scaled to 4 decimal places (`DECIMAL(18,4)`), with `RoundingMode.HALF_UP`.
- **Total Cost**: $\text{Total Cost} = \text{Direct Cost} + \text{Indirect Cost}$
- **Gross Profit**: $\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Total Cost}$
- **Gross Margin %**: $\text{Gross Margin \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Revenue}}\right) \times 100$
  - Safe zero-division guard: When $\text{Revenue} \le 0$, safely returns `0.0000%`, preventing `ArithmeticException`, `NaN`, or `Infinity`.
- **Cost Variance**: $\text{Cost Variance} = \text{Actual Cost} - \text{Baseline Cost}$ (when baseline exists).

### 7. REVENUE PROVENANCE & COST ATTRIBUTION
- **Traceability**: Every revenue number tracks `canonicalSourceType`, `canonicalSourceId`, `recognitionState`, and timestamp.
- **Cost Attribution**: Cost entries track `sourceType`, `sourceId`, `componentType`, `attributionBasis`, and dimension IDs (`jobId`, `productId`, `customerId`, `vendorId`).
- **Duplicate Prevention**: `ProfitabilitySourceRegistry` detects and flags duplicate revenue and cost attribution keys.

---

### 8. RECONCILIATION FOUNDATION
`ProfitabilityReconciliationService` performs non-mutating mathematical checks:
1. Verifies snapshot revenue matches the sum of its revenue provenances ($\Delta \le 0.0001$).
2. Verifies snapshot total cost matches the sum of its cost attributions ($\Delta \le 0.0001$).
3. Verifies cost breakdown sum matches total cost.
4. Cross-checks Module 15 period handoff ledger balance.
5. Emits `ProfitabilityReconciliationEvent` recording discrepancies without altering canonical data.

### 9. SECURITY, TENANT ISOLATION & RBAC
- **Multi-Tenancy**: Tenant ID (`tenant_id`) and Project ID (`project_id`) enforced across domain services, use cases, and SQL queries.
- **PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS)**:
  - Tables: `profitability_analysis_snapshots`, `profitability_cost_attributions`, `profitability_revenue_provenances`, `profitability_reconciliation_events`, `profitability_audit_events`.
  - Configured with `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, and `CURRENT_SETTING('app.current_tenant', true)` tenant isolation policies.
- **Server-Side RBAC**:
  - `ADMIN`, `MANAGER`, `STAFF`: Authenticated and authorized for analytical snapshot generation, retrieval, and source readiness.
  - `ADMIN`, `MANAGER`: Authorized for financial handoffs, snapshot reconciliation, and audit event inspection.
  - `CUSTOMER`, `GUEST`, `AFFILIATE`: Explicitly denied access (HTTP 403 Forbidden).
