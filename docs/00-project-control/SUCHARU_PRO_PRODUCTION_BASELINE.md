## 3. Protected Architectural & Business Boundaries

1. **Canonical Modules 00–24:** The 25 canonical ERP module boundaries and domain ownership must remain intact without merging or splitting.
2. **ERP as System of Record:** Database-driven financial, order, production, and inventory state cannot be overridden by UI-side or AI-side calculations.
3. **PostgreSQL Row-Level Security:** `ALTER TABLE ... ENABLE/FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with `app.current_tenant_id` must remain forced across all tables.
4. **Commercial Authority (`KNOW-CONF-004`):** Internal vendor purchase rates, paper substrate supplier discounts, and gross margins remain 100% protected and unexposed.
5. **Human Confirmation Gate (MCP Risk Policy R0–R3):** High-risk actions (`record_customer_payment`, `accept_and_lock_quotation`) generate `CopilotActionProposal` with `isConfirmationRequired = true` requiring explicit human staff approval.
6. **No Client-Side Hardcoded AI Responses:** AI natural-language answers are dynamically generated by Gemini LLM based on assembled context; no static Bengali response templates exist in application code.
